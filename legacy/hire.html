FAQ

Straight answers

How do we start?

Hit Start a project, pick WhatsApp, Telegram, or email. Share goals, timeline, and any links (Figma, old site, competitors). I’ll reply with fit and next steps.

Do you work with clients outside Nigeria?

Yes. Remote-friendly. Payment can be discussed (local transfer, Wise, crypto in some cases — we’ll agree in writing).

What stack do you prefer?

HTML/CSS/JS, React, TypeScript, Tailwind, modern hosting (Netlify, Vercel, etc.). Happy to adapt to your stack when it makes sense.

How long does a typical site take?

Landing / small brand site: often 1–3 weeks. Larger marketing site: 3–6 weeks. MVP web apps: usually 4–10+ weeks depending on scope.

Do you write backend / full stack?

Frontend and product UI is the core strength. I also work with Supabase/Firebase/APIs and can collaborate with your backend engineer — or scope full-stack when the product fits.

What about revisions and ownership?

Revision rounds are defined in the quote. You own the delivered code and assets for work you paid for, unless we agree open-source or shared IP for a product collaboration.
