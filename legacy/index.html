How I work

Steps I follow on every project

Clear process so you always know what’s happening — from first call to launch and beyond.

01

Discovery

Understand your goals, audience, and constraints. What problem are we solving? What does success look like?

02

Strategy

Map the approach: tech stack, timeline, and assumptions. Clear plan before a single line of code.

03

Build

Clean, intentional development focused on performance, accessibility, and maintainability — with regular updates.

04

Launch & scale

Ship to production, monitor, iterate on feedback. Built for long-term partnership, not one-off drop-offs.

About

I ship things people can click on Monday morning

I grew up curious about how the web could feel as intentional as a well-told story. That curiosity became a craft: clean interfaces, honest performance, and products that don't need a manual.

By day I build for clients — shops, ministries, brands that need to look credible and convert. By night I ship my own SaaS: tools I wish existed when I needed them.

I'm not chasing every trend. I care about clarity, speed, and whether a real person in Lagos on a mid-range phone can use what I built without rage-quitting.

What I'm not for

  • Endless redesigns with no decision-maker
  • “Make it look like Apple” without a product story
  • Projects that treat accessibility as optional
  • Scope that ignores mobile users in Africa
