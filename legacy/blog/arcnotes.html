Featured product · Blog

ArcNotes: time back from documents

Live at arcnotes.pxxl.click — AI that turns long PDFs into summaries, insights, and actions.

What it solves

People don’t fail because they can’t read — they fail because they can’t finish. Research papers, contracts, and reports pile up. ArcNotes compresses that pile into something scannable without throwing away the signal.

Who uses it & how

  • Students and researchers reviewing dense PDFs
  • Founders and ops teams skimming reports before meetings
  • Anyone who needs exportable notes, not another chat toy

The happy path is boring on purpose: upload → summary → insights → export. Product UI that stays quiet so the document stays the hero.

Why this matters if you hire me

I don’t only paint marketing pages. I ship tools with real workflows, loading states, and export paths. If you need a web app that feels intentional — not a template with a login — this is the muscle I bring.
